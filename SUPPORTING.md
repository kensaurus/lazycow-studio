# Support Lazycow Studio

Lazycow Studio is free and open source, and it stays that way. If it saves you evenings of editing, you can pay for its
development. Paying unlocks nothing in the app. The monthly plans come with a few small, real benefits around the
project itself.

### How the benefits work

- **Your GitHub username.** Checkout asks for it (optional). We add the label for your plan (`supporter`, `backer` or
  `sponsor`) to the issues you open, within a few days. Without a username we cannot match your issues.
- **Priority means looked at first.** It is not a promise to build a feature or to build it by a date. A request still
  has to fit what Lazycow Studio is for.
- **Sponsor logos.** Reply to your Stripe receipt email with an SVG or PNG. We may decline a logo or link that is
  unlawful or misleading.
- **The monthly vote** runs in GitHub Discussions; Backers and Sponsors get the link.
